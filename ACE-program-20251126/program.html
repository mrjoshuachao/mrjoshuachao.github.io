ACE Ideation Program

ACE Ideation Program is the warm-up cycle of your Applied Creativity Engine.

This program is where curiosity wakes up after years of following the rules. You’ll dream up “impossible” products by remixing ideas that don’t usually belong together, build stories through your customers’ eyes, and even create your own brand commercial just like a creative director.

It’s hands-on, fast-paced, and seriously fun. Every exercise is crafted to reshape how you think, create, and see the world around you. And here’s where the magic happens: as you play, you’ll start to co-create with AI. You’ll learn to orchestrate multiple AI tools like a conductor, amplifying your intelligence without ever losing your creativity or critical thinking.

Because creativity isn’t something that disappears, it’s a muscle you can always train. The ACE Ideation Program invites you to play, learn, and reawaken your creative engine.

ACE 101
Impossible Product Design Made Possible

Online Self-Paced Course:
● 7 Instruction Videos.
● Small assignments after each video.
● Final mini project work submission.

Overview: In this workshop, students conceptualize an imaginative product, service, or experience by combining elements from two existing ideas. They learn to craft a user story from the perspective of a potential customer and develop a branding strategy that communicates the product’s value within a real-world context.

Focus: ● Understand “Remixing” strategies for innovative ideation. ● Brainstorm creative “impossible products” by merging existing concepts. ● Develop skills in leveraging AI tools for product ideation, concept realization, branding, graphic design, and copywriting. ● Practice utilizing AI tools for visual narratives of an imaginary user journey. ● Prepare compelling Keynote/PowerPoint presentations to articulate brand stories.

ACE Innovation Program

After your training in the ACE Ideation Program, your creative muscles are strong, and now it’s time to put them in motion.

This is the ACE Innovation Program, the full cycle of your Applied Creativity Engine. Here, you’ll take the creative strength you’ve built in the Ideation Program and apply it to solve real-world problems, uncovering hidden opportunities and creating innovations that bring real value to people and brands.

You’ll step into the world, learn to empathize and see through others’ eyes, and discover what truly matters. You’ll learn how to co-think, co-reason, and collaborate with AI to turn your insights into action, developing a brand-relevant product concept and crafting a pitch through effective storytelling that could inspire corporate leaders themselves.

You’ll continue to orchestrate multiple tools like a conductor, amplifying your intelligence while keeping your creativity and judgment in command.

By the end, your creative engine will be fully activated. And with every new cycle, you’ll be able to turn ideas into real innovations that make a lasting impact, bridging the gap between imagination and reality.

ACE 102
The MVP Pitch Room

Online Self-Paced Course:
● 7 Instruction Videos.
● Small assignments after each video.
● Final mini project submission.

Overview: Each student will develop an innovative product concept for a notable company, running through a speed track that combined Design Thinking, Lean Startup principles, Google Design Sprint, and the Brand Pyramid model to turn product vision into pitch-ready product MVP concept that aligns with the company’s brand DNA.

Focus: ● Generate innovative and feasible product concepts through SCAMPER-based cross-industry brainstorming and AI-enhanced ideation. ● Define target user problems clearly and formulate user personas. ● Leverage AI tools to design effective survey, interview, and observational frameworks. ● Collect and analyze real or simulated user data to build empathy maps. ● Derive actionable insights, clearly state the refined and decomposed problem statements that lead to MVP features.

Online Self-Paced Course:
● 7 Instruction Videos.
● Small assignments after each video.
● Final presentation submission (files and videos.)

Overview: Teams explore the art of modern-day pitch design, learning how to structure a compelling, research-backed narrative. They will practice how to refine their product vision and complete a full investor-style pitch deck. Each student will deliver a final pitch presentation as if speaking directly to corporate sponsors.

Focus: ● Refine core MVP features for clear product differentiation and market positioning. ● Design presentation narrative and outline by analyzing modern-day pitch flow. ● Finalize MVP feature sets and perceptual map through AI-assisted brainstorming and visualization. ● Compose a concise and compelling 15-page business pitch proposal. ● Compose a 30-second teaser video to complement their presentation. ● Final presentation: Confidently deliver persuasive business pitches.
