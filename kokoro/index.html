Enroll in Health Insurance & Pension

For Japan's public health insurance and national pension system
High Priority

Overview

Why it mattersEnsures you and your family can access healthcare and contribute to the public pension.
When to doWithin 14 days after your move-in registration.
Estimated time30–45 minutes at the town hall.
Bring with youMy Number card, residence card, juminhyo, and bank book.

How the Japanese System Works

  1. Resident registration first

    Enrollment is automatic once you complete town hall registration.

  2. Choose your insurance type

    Employer-based (kenkō hoken) vs. national (kokumin kenkō hoken).

  3. Set up monthly payment

    Bank withdrawal recommended — first invoice arrives in 4–6 weeks.

  4. Receive your insurance card

    Mailed in ~2 weeks. Use it at any clinic for 70% co-pay coverage.

Common Mistakes

  • Waiting more than 14 days after move-in (back-payments may apply).
  • Not enrolling family members on the same day.
  • Forgetting to notify changes (job change, address) within 14 days.
