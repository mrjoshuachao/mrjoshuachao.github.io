Why are professionals, teachers, and students quietl​y adopting ACE?

Until now, we have only taught ACE, the Applied Creativity Engine, in small, invite-only workshops. More than 500 learners in Japan and Taiwan have already embedded this creative Engine into their mind, unlocking breakthroughs in creativity and productivity to thrive in the AI era. Word of mouth has taken ACE far beyond what we ever imagined. That is why, for the first time, we are opening our programs online to the public.

This is what happens when people amplify their creativity with ACE embedded in their mindset.

The world is changing faster than we can learn. Jobs are being reshaped before schools can adjust, and AI can already think, write, and design with greater speed and efficiency. The real danger is not that AI becomes smarter. The danger is that we stop thinking for ourselves.

This is why ACE was created.

ACE, the Applied Creativity Engine, is more than a program. It is a structured and AI supported learning cycle that builds a creative engine inside your mind. It strengthens your creativity, sharpens your critical thinking, and helps you take ownership of your learning for life.

It is not about memorizing tools or perfecting prompts. It is about learning to co-create with AI and turning it into an amplifier rather than a replacement.
