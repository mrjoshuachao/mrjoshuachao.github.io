ACE Ideation Program

ACE Ideation Program is the warm-up cycle of your Applied Creativity Engine.

This program is where curiosity wakes up after years of following the rules. You’ll dream up “impossible” products by remixing ideas that don’t usually belong together, build stories through your customers’ eyes, and even create your own brand commercial just like a creative director.

It’s hands-on, fast-paced, and seriously fun. Every exercise is crafted to reshape how you think, create, and see the world around you. And here’s where the magic happens: as you play, you’ll start to co-create with AI. You’ll learn to orchestrate multiple AI tools like a conductor, amplifying your intelligence without ever losing your creativity or critical thinking.

Because creativity isn’t something that disappears, it’s a muscle you can always train. The ACE Ideation Program invites you to play, learn, and reawaken your creative engine.

ACE Innovation Program

After your training in the ACE Ideation Program, your creative muscles are strong, and now it’s time to put them in motion.

This is the ACE Innovation Program, the full cycle of your Applied Creativity Engine. Here, you’ll take the creative strength you’ve built in the Ideation Program and apply it to solve real-world problems, uncovering hidden opportunities and creating innovations that bring real value to people and brands.

You’ll step into the world, learn to empathize and see through others’ eyes, and discover what truly matters. You’ll learn how to co-think, co-reason, and collaborate with AI to turn your insights into action, developing a brand-relevant product concept and crafting a pitch through effective storytelling that could inspire corporate leaders themselves.

You’ll continue to orchestrate multiple tools like a conductor, amplifying your intelligence while keeping your creativity and judgment in command.

By the end, your creative engine will be fully activated. And with every new cycle, you’ll be able to turn ideas into real innovations that make a lasting impact, bridging the gap between imagination and reality.
